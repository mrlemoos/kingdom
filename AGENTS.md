## Learned User Preferences

- British spelling in user-facing plugin messages and documentation.
- Use TDD for domain logic: write failing tests first, then implement until `mvn test` passes.
- Run `mvn package` and provide the JAR path when the user intends to deploy to the game server.
- Use grill-me or grill-with-docs for major feature design; ask one question at a time with a recommended answer; capture agreed domain terms in `CONTEXT.md` (glossary only, no implementation detail).
- Do not create git commits or pushes unless explicitly requested.
- Prefer tracer-bullet vertical slices over large single implementations; minimise diff scope and match existing Java/Maven conventions in the repo.
- Work directly on `main` in the primary working directory; do not create git worktrees. Subagents run in place, so run them one at a time when their changes would overlap.
- Use caveman mode (terse responses) when the user invokes `/caveman`; resume only after they say "stop caveman" or "normal mode".
- **Open PvP** for now: do not implement PvP restrictions, trial arenas, or war-combat damage gating until explicitly requested; vanilla player PvP stays on.
- Do not implement anti-AFK detection for life events.
- Eclipse JDT null analysis applies on Java edits: avoid `Optional.map` method references on `@Nonnull` receivers; guard with `isPresent()`/`isEmpty()`; run `ReadLints` on touched files.
- Prefer Bukkit/Paper built-in persistence over custom plugin storage when the platform already provides it (e.g. server `whitelist.json`); prison sentences must never remove a player from the server whitelist.

- Admin-defined kingdoms; players join once via `/kingdom join`; only OP can move members. `/kingdom info` surfaces war, police, and loyalty alongside existing kingdom/economy summaries.
- Noble titles are admin-assigned with fixed slots; Knight has unlimited slots; hierarchy sort in `/kingdom info`; title ladder: King/Queen, Prince/Princess (2 slots, yellow prefix, −3% default tax), Premier, Speaker, Duke/Duchess, Lord/Lady, Count/Countess, MP, Knight/Dame. Elections fill 8 Commons seats (up to 4 player MPs + villager professions, with **[MP] Citizen** backfill when professions run short; `none`/Commoner excluded from ranked seats and only backfills empty slots); OP cannot assign MP. **Premier** is elected by seated player MPs after a general election; when no player MPs are seated—or a player-only Premier election fails with none seated—a **Premier villager** is chosen from profession MPs (highest election-scan count; Citizen backfill ineligible), tables inaugural FISCAL then BUDGET bills two in-game days after the general election closes (divided by the villager Speaker), and shows a `[Premier]` nametag; monarch still grants royal assent in Lords. Villager MPs claim territory villagers and release them to stored origin with default despawn and combat behaviour when elections end; claiming wakes sleeping MPs and resets lying pose to standing on Commons seating; all villagers including seated villager MPs are damageable. **`/resign`** lets seated Premier or player MPs offer to leave; villager MP/Premier resignation uses the same pending-approval flow by targeting the seated villager entity (seated MP, Premier, or Speaker may offer). Office-holder keeps full parliamentary powers until King/Queen—or Prince when no monarch is seated—accepts; a **resignation letter** (paper) is delivered to the Crown inventory on offer (on login if offline); right-click opens the review GUI with Lords hub fallback; rejection leaves them in office; one pending offer per kingdom. Premier parliamentary actions are blocked during active elections. A **villager Speaker** presides whenever no player holds the SPEAKER rank: spawned fresh (never claims a territory villager) at the Speaker's Chair (`/kingdom parliament set speaker-chair`, falling back to the Commons point), `[Speaker]` nametag with no profession label, seated/dismissed and division business driven by the `ElectionTask` sweep; it opens a division as soon as a bill is tabled, closes it after `parliament.villager-speaker.division-window-days` (default 1) or at once when no player MPs are seated, and casts **nay** on a tie (Denison's rule, so tied bills fail); not resignable. Realm-handled division and the Premier villager's casting vote are retired (`docs/adr/0002`). Election ties with no player Speaker resolve by earliest nomination instead of entering `AWAITING_SPEAKER_TIE`. Acceptance triggers a Premier election or Commons by-election as appropriate.
- Noble chat/tab/nametag prefixes are bold uppercase with per-rank colours; citizens have no prefix; villager MP nametags use the same grey `[MP] ` prefix as player MPs (Premier villager uses `[Premier]` in Premier rank colour); other territory villagers show profession nametags (**Commoner** when none; **`[on strike]`** when a frozen wallet hits the strike threshold), reconciled on career change, chunk load, and villager spawn. Ordinary territory villagers (not seated MPs or Treasury Lords) inside a kingdom's linked WorldGuard region are protected from vanilla despawning; protection is position-scoped and reverts when they leave; reconciled on startup sync, chunk load, villager spawn, and a 60-second periodic sweep.
- **Parliament business**: general election prorogues (`ParliamentState.prorogue()`) until State Opening (Speech from the Throne paper → `StateOpeningGui`, or royal commission). **Hansard** records each closed division to `data.yml` and shelves volumes via `HansardArchivist` / `RegistrarShelfWriter`. **Motion of no confidence** (`BillType.NO_CONFIDENCE`) needs table + second by distinct seated player MPs (not the Premier); decides in Commons only; carrying removes the Premier and calls a `PREMIER` election without proroguing. **Referendum** (`BillType.REFERENDUM`) is advisory whole-realm polling (not a Commons division); holds the order paper for the polling window.
- **City**: monarch sets a **capital** (= city hall) with `/kingdom capital set` inside linked territory, cleared by `/kingdom capital clear`; a **Lord Mayor** wolf NPC (tamed, sitting, invulnerable, no AI, persistent, `Lord Mayor` nametag, PDC-tagged, no economy ties) stands there and is reconciled by the 60s territory sweep and on startup. **Build permits** are free, auto-granted kingdom-wide via the apply GUI on right-clicking the Mayor, and required to place/break blocks inside linked territory. Members only — foreigners can never hold one; King/Queen and Prince/Princess are exempt *in their own kingdom only*; **OP is not exempt** (deliberately unlike the Act-ban gate in `BuildConductEnforcer`). No capital → enforcement entirely off for that kingdom; unclaimed land is always free to build. Unlicensed building is refused (throttled ~30s per player) but is **not a crime** — no warrant, no loyalty drop. Prison and `/kingdom move` revoke; release does not restore (re-apply). Monarch also holds `/kingdom permit grant|revoke <player>` and a shift-right-click paginated **permit register** GUI with revoke confirm and a **State of the Realm** statistics item (subjects, permit holders, villagers, treasury, realm wealth, GDP/day, tax revenue, trades last day). See `docs/adr/0003-build-permits-issued-by-a-lord-mayor.md` and `CONTEXT.md` `## City`.
