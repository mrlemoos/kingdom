# Unsold gilts go to the issuing monarch

At the end of a gilt sale, the unsold face value becomes a full personal holding of the King or Queen who opened it. We chose that Crown gift over cancelling unsold bonds: it pays the same coupons and principal as a purchased holding, although the treasury received no Corona for it. The liability counts in national debt and reduces realm wealth; the BUDGET Act caps the issue's total face value at its approved spending allowance.
